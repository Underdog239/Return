？ How to use

Getting started

  1. Run a Minecraft Java Edition server (vanilla, Paper, etc. — up to 1.21.11) or open a singleplayer world to LAN.
  2. Click New Bot, point it at your server and pick a brain. With Ollama installed everything runs 100% offline.
  3. Watch the camera feed and talk to your bot in the chat panel.

Talking to the bot

  • Plain language goes to the bot's AI mind — it thinks, replies, and picks actions: “go find diamonds”.
  • Commands run instantly without the AI: !collectBlocks("oak_log", 10), !pvp("@hostile"), !goToCoordinates(100, 64, -200).
  • Autonomous mode gives it a goal it works on by itself: !selfPrompt("build a farm").

Smart movement

  • Navigation automatically sprints, jumps, breaks obstacles, bridges gaps with blocks from the bot's inventory, and finds alternative routes when blocked.
  • !bridgeTo(x, y, z) forces manual sneaky-bridging across ravines.

Fighting & gathering

  • !pvp("@hostile") / !pvp("@player") / !pvp("Steve") — advanced combat: strafing, crits, retreat & eat, creeper dodging.
  • !hunt(5) — hunts animals, cooks meat. !collectBlocks("stone", 64) — mines with best tools.

Camera

  • The camera opens on the viewer port (3000+) once the bot is in game. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, press P for first-person.
